    About this app

    Home Tracker keeps everything for the house in one place, so nothing renews or runs out without you knowing.

    • Renewals: insurance, broadband, energy and anything else, with who you're with, what it costs, past providers and reminders before it's due.
    • Car: MOT, service, tax, insurance history and the finance agreement.
    • Monthly budget: money in and out, your own groups, monthly or one-off items, spending against a budget (like Food), payment plans and expiry reminders.
    • Documents: photos and PDFs of policies and receipts, kept privately.

    It's private: only people you invite can see your home. It works on your phone and laptop and everything syncs as you go. Holiday Tracker uses the same account and sharing.
